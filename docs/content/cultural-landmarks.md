# Cultural landmarks

> Civilization-wide cultural events that touched fashion.

Political, social, technological, and cultural turning points that left a measurable mark on the fashion industry, on design vocabulary, or on how houses operated. Used as shared anchors across every house's timeline so each maison's story sits inside the broader world it lived in.

Status legend: **yes** = confirmed in, **maybe** = under consideration, **no** = considered and dropped.

## Confirmed

| Year | Event | Why it matters for fashion | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1851 | Singer sewing machine | Domestic + factory mass production becomes possible; ready-to-wear industry begins. | yes |
| 1938 | Nylon synthesised (DuPont) | Synthetic fibres reshape what couture and ready-to-wear can do — stockings, parachutes, then mass apparel. | yes |
| 1939-45 | World War II / end of the war | Fabric rationing, Paris under occupation, couture paralysed; the 1945 liberation triggers the postwar reset (US-led ready-to-wear rise, then Dior's 1947 New Look). | yes |
